# Privacy Policy

**Flirty** ("the app") is built to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect

Flirty has no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data.

## On-device processing (default)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, every reply is generated entirely on your device using Apple's Foundation Models. The messages you paste, the notes you keep, and the replies Flirty writes never leave your phone. Screenshots you pick are read on device with Apple's Vision framework; the image itself is never stored or sent anywhere, and Flirty does not get access to your photo library beyond the picture you choose.

## Cloud mode (optional, consent-based)

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Flirty offers an optional cloud mode. It is off by default and asks for your consent first. If you allow it, the message you paste (or the text extracted from a screenshot), your notes about the person, your optional context, and a short summary of earlier exchanges are sent over HTTPS to the Flirty gateway, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude model to write the reply. No other data is sent, and nothing is stored server-side beyond what is needed to serve that single request. You can turn cloud mode off at any time from the cloud icon on the main screen.

## Local storage

Profiles, notes, and conversation history are stored locally on your device only. They are never synced or uploaded. Deleting a profile deletes its history.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only external service Flirty ever contacts is the Flirty gateway described above, and only when you have explicitly allowed cloud mode.
